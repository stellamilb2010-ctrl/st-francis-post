The Rise of AI: Opportunity or Threat?

By Laura Moreira · September 16, 2025

It’s 2:00 a.m a student is sitting at her desk with a blank document. With a tight deadline approaching, instead of brainstorming or searching for information, she simply opens up an AI tool, inputs the prompt she received from her teachers, and an entire essay appears on her computer in a matter of seconds. This is occurring more frequently now than ever before; therefore, the introduction of artificial intelligence is fundamentally changing how we think, work and create.

OpenAI helps lead this change by creating programs that allow an individual to write essays, answer difficult questions, create hyper-realistic images, etc., in a matter of seconds. Once-reliable tools of electronic writing and the like now play significant roles in everyday life. As artificial intelligence continues to develop rapidly, the question is no longer if artificial intelligence will change how we live but rather to what degree having artificial intelligence has changed how we live. The more pointed question will be whether or not changes as a result of the invention of artificial intelligence will be positive for our lives.

There are numerous ways to see the positive effect of using AI for everyone. AI saves time, improves efficiencies and gives people a way to do things that would be difficult without the use of AI. For instance, students are able to use AI to help them better organize their thoughts and understand difficult subjects. Employers use AI to automate repetitive tasks, thereby reducing their costs and increasing worker productivity. In the field of medicine, AI helps doctors identify diseases earlier than otherwise possible, which could help save lives. Consequently, it should be obvious that AI represents vast improvements over where we currently are.

However, the benefits of A.I. do carry some costs. As A.I. gets better and better at quickly generating essays, artwork and communicating just like, or better than a human being; people are starting to ask what does that say about the originality/authenticity of what they have created? With all the new, powerful tools available to everyone to create all types of things; how can an individual claim their creation as “theirs?” The effects of these incursions on our perceptions and understanding of the creative process are felt throughout the educational spectrum; therefore, artificial intelligence has blurred the distinction between education and outsourcing of human thought.

The potential downside of artificial intelligence in education does extend to society. The potential for AI-generated content being misused to produce inappropriate information and/or produce “fake news” that causes people to have increased difficulty discerning between fact and fiction is a definite possibility. As a result of the current mistrust in the media, adding this technology would only exacerbate the effects of this phenomena. Moreover, as advancements in technology continue to decimate current jobs, there are serious questions surrounding where humans will fit in the new workforce.

Another serious issue is determining the proper control of artificial intelligence technologies. The pace of technological advancement has far exceeded the ability for the regulatory framework developed to ensure safe technological development to keep up with the pace of advancement of said technologies. With corporations competing for dominance by developing more advanced artificial intelligence systems, there will be difficulties associated with the implementation of these developed technologies in ways that are not in the best interest of society.

Artificial intelligence is now an integral part of being human, and as such, it will become even more invasive into the lives and activities of humans as time goes on. The challenge will not be to eradicate the use of AI but to learn how to effectively use artificial intelligence without developing excessive dependence upon the technology.

To summarise, the development of artificial intelligence technologies is more important than just the technology; it is about being human. The emergence of AI is forcing society to examine, again, its definitions of creativity, trust and of intelligence, generally. Ultimately, the impact of AI on our lives will be determined as much by the technology developed by AI as by the decisions made by the users of AI concerning the use, or non-use, of artificial intelligence.
